Three things that need your eyes specifically
---------------------------------------------

1) `experiments/03_real_pl_all_semantics_in_lean/expt_v1/maude/`
       `pythonlite-bigstep.maude`
   Read this next to
       `cs522/HW3-Maude/imp/0-imp-original/1-imp-bigstep/imp-semantics-bigstep.maude`
   The judgment shapes are the same Kahn-style
       < Exp,  Sigma, Out >  =>  < Value, Out' >
       < Stmt, Sigma, Out >  =>  < Outcome, Out' >
       < Pgm                >  =>  < Sigma, Out >
   Three additions over IMP++:
     * a Value sort (Int ⊔ Bool ⊔ String ⊔ none ⊔ listV ⊔ tupV ⊔ funcV)
     * an Outcome sort (State ⊔ ret(V) ⊔ raised(V)) for
       early returns and exceptions, propagated through seq/if/while
     * a captured-State field on funcV for lexical closures
   Question for you: do those three additions stay faithful to CS522
   big-step style, or did the LLM invent something off-pattern?

2) `experiments/03_real_pl_all_semantics_in_lean/expt_v1/verification_spec.md`
   The three-layer protocol we proposed for certifying the output:
     (a) differential testing vs reference interpreter   [29/29 PASS]
     (b) cross-framework equivalence theorems in Lean    [deferred]
     (c) textbook + your spot-check                       [pending you]
   Is layer (a) at this scale enough for you to sign off on the
   "behaviour" half of correctness?  And if not, what corpus / what
   extra layer would convince you?

3) `experiments/03_real_pl_all_semantics_in_lean/expt_v1/maude/results/results_summary_2026-04-26__21-18-11.md`
   Full per-test rewrite-count table + a partial honesty inventory
   of the bugs the iterative testing caught (Maude's parser
   surprised the LLM repeatedly — undeclared trailing-apostrophe
   vars, inline V:Value annotations not matching, `(...)` inside
   `---` comments breaking the lexer, `Sigma(X) ==Bool listV(L)` is
   not a matching condition, etc.).
